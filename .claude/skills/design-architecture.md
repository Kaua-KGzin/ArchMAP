name: design-architecture
description: Designs clean and scalable system architecture. Use when planning projects, structuring codebases, or improving organization.
---

When designing architecture:

1. **Clarify requirements**: Inputs, outputs, scale, constraints
2. **Define components**: Break system into modules/services
3. **Show structure**: Use diagrams or folder structures
4. **Explain interactions**: How components communicate
5. **Apply best practices**: SOLID, separation of concerns
6. **Consider scalability**: Future growth and maintenance

Keep designs simple and practical.
